Why MoonBite

Fair by design

⚖️

No premine, fair launch

Every MBITE is mined in the open under one published emission schedule. No insider allocation, no hidden head-start.

🔐

Self-custody first

Non-custodial by default. We never hold your keys — and the site never asks you for a seed phrase or private key.

🧾

Verifiable & open

Litecoin-lineage core, a public explorer and open tooling. Don't trust us — check the chain yourself.

Tokenomics

A supply you can count on.

Every MBITE enters through one public emission schedule — no premine, no insider unlocks. The block reward starts at 10 MBITE and halves every 700,000 blocks, so issuance bends toward a hard asymptotic cap of 13,999,999.909 MBITE and never crosses it.
